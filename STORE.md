# Store Copy — Clipboard Buddy

## App Store (English)

**Name:** Clipboard Buddy  
**Subtitle:** History, tags & favorites  
**Keywords:** clipboard,pasteboard,history,snippets,menu bar,password,tags,favorites  

**Description:**

Clipboard Buddy keeps everything you copy — text and images — in a searchable history on your Mac.

Automatically tags URLs, passwords, tokens, hashes, and more. Sensitive items stay blurred until you unlock with Touch ID or your password. Pin favorites to the menu bar for one-click copy.

Built for macOS with Dark Mode, VoiceOver, and Dynamic Type support.

**What's New:** Initial release.

**Privacy nutrition (summary):** Clipboard contents stay on device. Optional analytics (no content). No tracking.
